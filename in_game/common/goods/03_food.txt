#intentionally is a replacement file, since this removes goods.
# These goods no longer make food; provisions does, from them, and never more
# food than they carried. The food value each good keeps below is the balancing
# lever: it sets the good's price per food against provisions' 0.100. The full
# table and the recipe rationale are at the top of production_provisions.txt.
#
#   wheat 14  fish 12  livestock 11  legumes 10  wild_game 9  fruit 5  olives 4
#
# Luxury demand on a good is a class's luxury food share (MnT_pop_types.txt)
# divided by that value. Wheat, fish and legumes are recipe inputs only, so
# only hunger pulls on them. Livestock, wild game, fruit and olives are also
# luxury demand, pulled by wealth. Fruit and olives never pay as the main
# ingredient: they are the small second ingredient in a meal, or a fifth of
# the grove's output becomes provisions at the RGO.
