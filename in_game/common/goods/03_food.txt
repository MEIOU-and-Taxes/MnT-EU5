# This is intentionally a replacement file, since this removes goods.

# These goods no longer make food; provisions do, and are produced by other
# food goods and never generate more food than those goods carried. The food
# value for each good is kept consistent at these values and sets the good's
# price per food against provisions' 0.100. The full table is at the top of
# production_provisions.txt.
#
#   wheat 14  fish 12  livestock 11  legumes 10  wild_game 9  fruit 5  olives 4
#
# Luxury demand on a good is a class's luxury food share (MnT_pop_types.txt)
# divided by that value. Wheat, fish and legumes are recipe inputs only, so
# only provisions production generates demand for them. Livestock, wild game,
# fruit and olives are also luxury demand. Fruit and olives are currently
# unprofitable as the main ingredient in provisions: they are the small second
# ingredient in a meal and some are converted into provisions as a side effect
# of rgo buildings but they don't make a ton of sense as a primary food source

}
