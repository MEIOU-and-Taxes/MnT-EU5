# Buildings that turn food goods into provisions, the only good that carries food.
# A recipe's provisions never carry more food than its inputs.
#
#   good        food   price   price per food   share of start supply
#   wheat        14    0.50    0.036            46%
#   fish         12    0.50    0.042            11%
#   livestock    11    0.75    0.068            24%
#   legumes      10    0.50    0.050             8%
#   wild_game     9    0.50    0.056             6%
#   fruit         5    0.75    0.150             4%
#   olives        4    0.75    0.188             1%
#   wool         11    1.25    0.114   RGO supplement only, never a recipe input
#   provisions   10    1.00    0.100
#
# A good is a profitable input below 0.100 per food, or below 0.075 when cooking loses a quarter of the food.
# Balance with the food values in 03_food.txt, not with prices.
# Supply share: owned food locations at start, weighted by development.
