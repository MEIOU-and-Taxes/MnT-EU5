# Combines food goods into provisions, the only good that yields food. Food is
# never created: a recipe's provisions carry at most the food its ingredients
# carried. A recipe's margin is set by what its inputs cost per food, what
# provisions sell for per food, and how much of the food survives the cooking.
#
#   good        food   price   price per food   share of start supply
#   wheat        14    0.50    0.036            46%
#   fish         12    0.50    0.042            11%
#   livestock    11    0.75    0.068            24%
#   legumes      10    0.50    0.050             8%
#   wild_game     9    0.50    0.056             6%
#   fruit         5    0.75    0.150             4%
#   olives        4    0.75    0.188             1%
#   wool         11    1.25    0.114   RGO supplement only, never a recipe input
#   provisions   10    1.00    0.100
#
# A good makes money as an ingredient when it costs under 0.100 per food, or
# under 0.075 where a quarter of the food is lost in cooking. Wheat, fish,
# legumes, game and livestock all pass both tests, livestock only just. Fruit
# and olives never pay as ingredients: they only appear as the small second
# ingredient in a bread recipe, or a fifth of the grove's output becomes
# provisions at the RGO. Wool stands for the flock's meat and milk: a fifth of
# it becomes provisions at the RGO and nowhere else. The food values in
# 03_food.txt are the balancing tool, prices are not. Supply shares are
# development-weighted counts of owned food locations at start.
#
# Each recipe has one main ingredient and one small second ingredient, so a
# scarce good is never used heavily by a recipe that only adds it for variety.
# Every recipe in a building takes the same food in, so automation, which
# picks by total profit, ranks them the same way their margins do.
#
# Recipes: the provisions market is the bulk trade, 250 laborers and 20 food
# of ingredients a level, keeping three quarters of the food. Wheat is the
# main ingredient in five staples because it is nearly half the food grown,
# with a little fish, legumes, meat, oil or fruit; fish is the main ingredient
# in two more, one with a little bread for the coasts and one with game for
# the north. Meat and bread turns that around for herding regions: livestock
# is the main ingredient with a little bread, low profit at base prices and
# high profit where livestock is cheap.
# The victualling house is the burgher trade, a hundred workers and 10 food a
# level, half the market's input, keeping all of it: bread or fish with a
# small amount of the more expensive meat, game, fruit or oil. Burghers make
# more per person, the market more per building.
#
# Margins: food is cheap next to provisions, so the recipes with nothing but
# food would make 100 percent. Firewood, pottery and salt are the kitchen
# costs that bring the market's main recipes to about 20 percent; the
# victualling house adds glass for the table and lands near 35. Recipes with
# livestock, game or fruit as the main ingredient make less than that at base
# prices and only pay where those goods are cheap.
