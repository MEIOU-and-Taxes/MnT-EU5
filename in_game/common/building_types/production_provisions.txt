# Combines food goods into provisions, the only good that yields food. Food is
# never created: a recipe's provisions carry at most the food its ingredients
# carried. A recipe's margin is set by what its inputs cost per food, what
# provisions sell for per food, and how much of the food survives the cooking.
#
#   good        food   price   price per food   share of start supply
#   wheat        14    0.50    0.036            46%
#   fish         12    0.50    0.042            11%
#   livestock    11    0.75    0.068            24%
#   legumes      10    0.50    0.050             8%
#   wild_game     9    0.50    0.056             6%
#   fruit         5    0.75    0.150             4%
#   olives        4    0.75    0.188             1%
#   wool         11    1.25    0.114   RGO supplement only, never a recipe input
#   provisions   10    1.00    0.100
#
# A good pays as an ingredient under 0.100 per food, or under 0.075 where a
# quarter of the food is lost in cooking. The food values in 03_food.txt are
# the balancing tool, prices are not. Supply shares are development-weighted
# counts of owned food locations at start.
